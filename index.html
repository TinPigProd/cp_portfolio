The philosophy
"Most organizations measure what gets done.
The best ones also measure how."

The What and The How aren't just tier labels in a competency framework. They're a philosophy about what makes performance sustainable — and what makes organizations worth staying in.

TinPigProd builds infrastructure for both dimensions, informed by Korn Ferry's behavioral science and Brené Brown's research on psychological safety and vulnerability in high-performing teams.
